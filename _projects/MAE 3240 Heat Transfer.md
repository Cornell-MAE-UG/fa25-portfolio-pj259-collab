One homework in Heat Transfer, MAE3240, that I found particularly valuable was Problem Set 3, where we derived the governing heat conduction equation for a rod using energy balance on differential control volume. In this assignment, I learned to apply the conservation of energy by accounting conduction, convection, internal heat generation and energy storage. The schematic models help me to visualize the type of heat transfer, along with the temperature nodes at  each point. This specific format of homework reinforced the importance of clearly starting assumptions and defining the control volume before beginning the analysis.

Instead of memorizing the equation, the homework allows me to work through and derive it by using fundamental knowledge. These skills could be useful in the future where governing equations are not given.
